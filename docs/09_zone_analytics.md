# Zone Analytics Module

## Objective

The Zone Analytics Module measures how customers interact with different areas of the store.

The goal is to convert visitor tracking information into business insights such as:

* Most visited store sections
* Customer dwell time
* High engagement areas
* Low engagement areas
* Customer movement patterns

---

## Floor A Zones

### Skincare Browsing

Wall-mounted skincare product displays.

### Premium Skincare

Higher-value skincare products and consultation area.

### Promo Walkway

Promotional display island and primary circulation path.

### Beauty Consultation

Mirror station and customer assistance area.

---

## Floor B Zones

### Beauty Station

Customer entry and beauty assistance area.

### Premium Makeup

Premium cosmetic brand displays.

### Browsing Corridor

Primary customer movement corridor.

### Color Cosmetics

Lipsticks, foundations, and makeup testing section.

---

## Analytics Generated

The system calculates:

* Zone visits
* Visitor dwell time
* Most popular zones
* Customer traffic distribution
